## The Problem (two linked failures)

### Failure 1: Homeowners cannot see the result before they commit

In a 79m² Dutch apartment (average CBS 2026), a 6cm scan error makes a sofa,
kitchen unit, or wardrobe simply not fit. Yet IKEA Kreativ — the only free mass-
market 3D tool in NL/BE — publishes a 6cm margin of error and explicitly tells
its own users NOT to use the scan for kitchens or bathrooms (IKEA Belgium support
pages). Professional 3D renders cost €200-5,000/image. DIY tools (Planner5D,
RoomSketcher) require 3-5 hours to learn. Homeowners spend 9.6 months planning
a kitchen renovation (Houzz 2024, 32,615 respondents) because they cannot get
certainty about the outcome. Kwantum (€622M Homefashion Group) sends humans to
measure customer homes for free — direct commercial proof the pain is real and
the current best solution is unscalable human labour.

### Failure 2: Homeowners cannot find a trustworthy contractor

A Rotterdam criminal court (June 2025, ECLI:NL:RBROT:2025:7704) convicted two
renovation fraudsters who recruited 18-55+ victims specifically via homedeal.nl
and werkspot.nl. Werkspot charges contractors per lead regardless of outcome;
contractors publicly post: "they go silent and I still pay the lead fee for
nothing." 6+ contacted contractors with zero quote received is a documented
complaint (r/Klussers). Belgium: a Ghent couple lost €284,000 to a bankrupt
contractor. The government legal advice body (Juridisch Loket) maintains a
dedicated "Problemen met aannemer" page with template legal letters — the
problem is institutionally recognised.

## The Hypothesis (falsifiable)

NL/BE homeowners planning a renovation will use a free AI 3D visualisation tool
to generate a before/after render of their renovation (accurate to within 2cm —
beating IKEA Kreativ's published 6cm ceiling), and renovation contractors will
pay €35 per pre-scoped, pre-visualised lead (vs €15-25 for a cold Werkspot lead)
because conversion rates for scoped leads are 7-9x higher than cold leads.

**This hypothesis is false if:**
(a) contractors will not pay a premium for pre-visualised, scoped leads, OR
(b) homeowners will not use a free 3D tool in sufficient numbers to generate
    lead volume before a better-resourced competitor enters the market

## Competitive moat

No NL/BE platform combines AI 3D visualisation + contractor matching (confirmed
across full competitive sweep, July 2026). Block Renovation (US) is the only
global proof-of-concept — zero EU presence. IKEA Kreativ's kitchen/bathroom
accuracy failure is a publicly stated competitive opening. Decodata (Amsterdam,
150K+ Dutch furniture SKUs) is an available partnership that eliminates 12-18
months of catalog-building.

## Entry wedge (Phase 1)

B2B via estate agents: Funda (837M annual visits) algorithmically ranks listings
higher for 3D tours. Estate agents have direct financial incentive to offer
accurate AI room scans. Offer scan + render at €19-49/property. Contractor
matching activates post-sale when buyer becomes a renovating homeowner. B2B sale
to 50-200 agents is achievable within €5K budget before any consumer marketing.
